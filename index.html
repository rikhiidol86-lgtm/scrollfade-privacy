ScrollFade Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Summary

ScrollFade helps you scroll less. It works on your phone, has no account system and no servers of its own, and never sells your data. Your usage stays on your device.

What ScrollFade uses on your phone

  • Usage Access: to know which app is open, so it can fade or pause the apps you chose. It does not see what you watch, type or read.
  • Display over other apps: to draw the fade, the step-away pill and the pause screen.
  • Camera (optional): only while you scan your Barcode Anchor or do the Movement toll, where on-device pose detection counts your squats or jumping jacks. Camera frames are analysed on the phone and never saved or sent.
  • Notifications: to show check-in and pass timers, milestones and the weekly reflection.
  • Device admin (optional): only to stop impulsive uninstalls when you turn on Uninstall Protection. It never locks, wipes or reads your device, and Android always lets you turn it off in system settings.
  • Accessibility (optional, Precise mode): only inside supported apps (Instagram and YouTube), to recognise the Reels or Shorts screen from the names of on-screen elements, so only that screen fades. It never reads your messages or what you watch, never types or taps for you, and nothing is saved or sent.
  • Call status: ScrollFade checks whether a voice or video call is active (not who you are talking to), so calls are never faded.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, daily usage counters, streaks, schedules and pledge milestones are stored only inside the app on your phone. ScrollFade has no servers and does not upload them anywhere.

Android may include them in your own Google device backup if you have backup turned on.

Deleting your data: open ScrollFade → Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

Reels-Free Web Portal

The Portal is a built-in browser for Instagram and YouTube. Pages load directly from those services, and your logins are kept in the browser storage on your phone. ScrollFade does not see your passwords or messages. Those services’ own privacy policies apply to what you do there. ScrollFade is not affiliated with Instagram, Meta, YouTube or Google.

Precise mode rules

If you use Precise mode, ScrollFade downloads a small public text file once a day from this website (GitHub Pages) with the current screen names it looks for, so it keeps working after Instagram or YouTube updates. Nothing about you is sent. Like any web request, GitHub can see your IP address.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the Today tab, a sponsored card in Insights and You, and optional reward videos you choose to watch to try one Pro feature for 24 hours.

Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about ad interactions, to show, measure and protect ads. See Google’s privacy policy.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first and can change it in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices. Ads never appear on the pause screen, the fade or the Portal. Plans without ads remove them all. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play. ScrollFade receives only a confirmation of what you bought, never your payment details.

Sharing

ScrollFade only shares something when you choose to, for example a progress card, an invite or a Commitment Code sent through your phone’s share menu.

Security

Network connections made by the app and its ad partners use encryption (HTTPS). Your Commitment Code is stored only as a salted hash.

Your choices and rights

  • See your data: everything ScrollFade keeps is shown in the app.
  • Delete it: Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the app.
  • Ads: change your ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices (where shown), and reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.

Because ScrollFade has no servers, we hold no personal data about you outside your phone. For any privacy request or question, email us and we will reply within 30 days. You can also contact the data protection authority where you live.
